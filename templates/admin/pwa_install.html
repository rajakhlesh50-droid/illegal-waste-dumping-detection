Mobile Phone Installation Guide
Android Phone (Chrome)
  • 1 Connect phone Wi-Fi to Laptop Hotspot or same Wi-Fi network.
  • 2 Open Camera or Google Lens and scan the QR code on the right.
  • 3 Tap Chrome's 3-dot menu icon (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  • 4 Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install App".
  • 5 Launch WasteAI anytime straight from your mobile apps screen!
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  • 1 Connect iPhone Wi-Fi to Laptop Hotspot or shared Wi-Fi.
  • 2 Scan QR code with the iOS Camera app and tap the link to open in Safari.
  • 3 Tap the Share button () at the bottom.
  • 4 Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ().
  • 5 Tap Add. WasteAI icon is now on your iPhone home screen!
